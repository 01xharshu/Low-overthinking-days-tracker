## MindCycle v0.1.0

**A gentle personal cycle tracker for Low Feeling and Overthinking days** — inspired by the iOS Health app's period tracking.

MindCycle helps you log low-feeling or overthinking days, spot patterns, see how long phases last, and get simple predictions for upcoming low windows. All your data stays **100% private and local** on your Mac — no cloud, no tracking, no account required.

### ✨ Features
- Easy logging of Low Feeling, Overthinking, or Both days with intensity and notes
- Beautiful interactive Swift Charts showing occurrence, frequency, intensity, and deviation over time
- Automatic cycle length calculation and upcoming low day predictions
- Clean Dashboard with quick log button and countdown to next expected low window
- macOS Widget (small, medium, large) — glanceable view of days since last low day + next predicted window (add it from Notification Center or Desktop)
- History with search and filters
- Export your data as JSON or CSV
- Calming dark-mode-first design with soft colors

### 📥 Installation (Free Unsigned Build)
1. Download `MindCycle-v0.1.0.dmg`
2. Open the DMG and drag **MindCycle.app** to your **Applications** folder
3. Double-click the app to launch

**⚠️ Important:** Because this is a free build (no paid Apple Developer certificate), macOS will show a warning:  
**"MindCycle cannot be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software."**

**How to open it:**
- Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**
- Scroll down to the Security section — you will see MindCycle mentioned
- Click **Open Anyway**
- Try launching the app again

This is completely normal for indie apps distributed for free on GitHub. The app is safe and contains no malware.

After launching once, you can add the **MindCycle widget** from the macOS Notification Center or Desktop.

### Known Limitations (v0.1)
- This is an early MVP built with Antigravity + manual tweaks
- Widget requires macOS 14+ (Sonoma or later)
- No automatic updates yet
- Pattern prediction is basic (will improve with more logged data)
